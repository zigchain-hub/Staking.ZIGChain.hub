Help Center
How do I stake ZIG?v
Click Start Staking, enter your amount, copy the deposit address, send ZIG, verify the amount, then begin staking.
What is the current APR?v
Current staking APR is 35.1% annually. Rewards accrue every block (~5.8 seconds).
What is the unbonding period?v
When you unstake there is a 30-day unbonding period. No rewards accrue during this time.
Will my data save if I close the browser?v
Yes — your staked amount and start time are saved locally. Your portfolio and rewards restore automatically on return.
What is Auto-Compound?v
Auto-compound automatically restakes your earned rewards daily, boosting your effective APR from 35.1% to approximately 36.8%.
